How to sign up ↓
/ Organization sign-up

Sign up in
three steps.

01

Get the app

Download MysMatch from the App Store or Google Play.

02

Choose your account type

Create an account and pick Partner / Service Provider. You can't change this later, so use a separate account from your personal one.

03

Set up your page

Add your name, logo and photos. Then post your first event, perk or job.

/ What you can do

One page.
Everything students need.

Events

Create events with capacity, pricing and QR tickets you scan at the door.

Perks

Offer discounts students redeem with a QR code or a simple in-store offer.

Jobs

Post student jobs, internships and mini-jobs, and receive applications with CVs.

Announcements

Reach your followers with updates right in the app.

Chat

Talk with students and applicants in one place.

Insights on the web

Sign in with Organization login to see followers, redemptions, events and attendees — and export lists.
