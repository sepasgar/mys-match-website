Rules & privacy.

The short version

  • Send one entry per track (Scenario, Creative post, Content creator) by 15 December 2026, 23:59 (Berlin time).
  • It must be your own original work.
  • The strongest entries are invited to interview for a role in our social media team. There is no cash prize.
  • We keep entries until 15 June 2027 and then delete them, together with all uploaded files.

Who can take part

Anyone aged 18 or over may enter. You can't take part if you live in a country under comprehensive EU or US sanctions (currently for example Iran, North Korea, Syria and Cuba). This is because we can't legally offer you a role or pay you there. Where you live counts, not your nationality.

Entries

  • One entry per person per track. You can edit or withdraw an entry until the deadline; after that entries are locked.
  • Videos are sent as a Google Drive link that anyone with the link can view. Please keep it available until 15 June 2027.
  • Don't include other people's personal data, music or footage you don't have the rights to, or anything offensive. We may remove such entries.

Your work and your rights

You keep the copyright of your entry. By entering you allow MysMatch to show your entry, credited to you, on its own channels (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, website and app), free of charge and without time limit. If we want to use your work commercially in any other way, we'll ask you first.

Judging

The MysMatch team judges the idea, the craft, and how well an entry fits our audience and channels. You can follow your status (Submitted, In review, Shortlisted, Interview, Selected / Not selected) on the challenge page after signing in. Being shortlisted or interviewed doesn't guarantee a job offer. Our decisions are final.

Privacy

What we collect: your account email, the details you type in the form (name, email, phone, city, country, links, handle, availability), your entry and the files you upload (CV, documents, images, PDFs).

Why: to run the challenge, judge entries, contact you about your entry and, if you're shortlisted, about a role. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who sees it: only you and the MysMatch team members who judge the challenge. Files are stored privately with our hosting provider (Supabase) and opened through short-lived links.

How long: until 15 June 2027. Withdrawing an entry or deleting your account removes it earlier, files included.

Your rights: you can see and change your entry on the challenge page, withdraw it, or ask us to access, correct or delete your data at contact@mys-match.com. More details are in our Privacy Policy.

Questions

Write to contact@mys-match.com or use the questions field in the form.
