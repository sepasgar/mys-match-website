Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

No endless swiping. No algorithms optimized for engagement. Just smart tools designed for students to find the right study partners.

Smart Discovery

Automatically discover students who share your university, major, and semester — organized in tiers from closest match to broadest.

Search Preferences

Set your own criteria — university, major, semester, city, or gender — and browse matching students. Your search, your way.

Instant Chat

Once matched, start chatting right away. Real-time messages with read receipts.

Student Events

Browse and join campus events, or create your own. See who's organizing, track your registrations, and get QR tickets on the day.

Biometric Security

Lock the app with Face ID, fingerprint, or a custom PIN. Your privacy is non-negotiable.

Report & Block

Instantly block or report anyone. We review every report and take action fast.

How it works

From sign-up to matched in minutes

1

Create Your Profile

Sign up with your university email. Add your photo, major, and a few details about yourself.

2

Search or Discover

Browse auto-matched study partners, or set your own search criteria: university, major, semester, city, and more.

3

Send a Match Request

Found someone interesting? Send a match request. They can accept or pass — no pressure.

4

Start Talking

Once both sides agree, a private chat opens. Plan a study session, group project, or campus meetup.
